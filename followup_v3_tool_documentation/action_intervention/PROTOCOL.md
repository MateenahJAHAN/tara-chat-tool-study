# Does one district-token activation change a generated call?

The earlier action responses and the block-14 judgment effects are known. No free-generation results for this activation swap have been inspected. This local protocol fixes the site, cards and endpoints before new generation. It reuses Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and the archived original inline compass bank. It does not add a random seed, layer search or interface choice after inspecting the new outputs.

## Cards and intervention

Use all eight services, all four requested districts and all four combinations of service and office match. Field order follows the original checkerboard across services and target districts, giving 128 distinct recipients. For each tool and target district, the four prompts are a matched quartet. A service donor flips the one service-district token and leaves the office and all other tokens intact. An office donor flips the one office token and leaves the service and all other tokens intact. Run both directions in all quartets. Verify the expected token identity at every donor and recipient address.

Generate complete responses in six conditions on each recipient: unmodified, block-14 identity at the service word, block-14 service donor, block-14 office donor, service embedding donor, and office embedding donor. At block 14 (1-based, of 28) or at the input embedding, replace exactly the indicated token's whole vector at prompt prefill, drawing the value from an unaltered donor in the same batch. Keep all other positions at that layer unchanged. Complete ordinary cached greedy decoding with no further swaps or logit manipulation. Use original checkpoint weights, tokenizer, rendering, float32, eager attention and the original 72-token limit. Store full token IDs, text, parser scores, winner and first-token gap over the runner-up, finish reason, donor and recipient IDs and site. No calls execute.

## Endpoint and controls

Primary example: an office-only match is out of scope. Move the office state toward the neither-matches donor. Count individual false calls prevented and introduced among the 32 office-only recipients. Compare with changing the actual office word, assessed by the unmodified donor response. Report both directions for the reciprocal neither-matches recipients. The two in-scope cells are positive demand controls. For each type of patch report how many originally exact in-scope calls were retained or lost and how many originally incorrect actions became exact, across all 64 in-scope recipients. Report the two out-of-scope cells separately, including any new false calls. For all cells report attempts, exact calls (function and requested district), literal abstentions, other and capped outputs using the original response parser.

A full positive control replaces the input embedding instead of the internal state. Because each donor differs at just one token, each resulting generated sequence should reproduce its unmodified donor. Compare unmodified responses to the original saved tokens; identity should reproduce the unmodified recipient. Test all four cells under both fields. Any differences in control tokens or scores will be reported. Determine whether they affect interpretation before claiming a change in action. An unchanged action under a block-14 swap supports a null result for that position and site on this finite bank, not equivalence across checkpoints, layers, tokens or templates. No test against a hypothetical population. Report runtime, device, hashes and condition counts.
